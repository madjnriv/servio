# Servio

> A mobile multi-service marketplace that connects users with trusted professionals for everyday services, repairs, maintenance, and skilled work.

## 📱 About

**Servio** is an MVP mobile marketplace built for **iOS and Android** that makes it easier for people to find and connect with service providers for different everyday needs.

Instead of searching for different providers for every task, users can discover and access multiple services from one place.

The initial vision includes services such as:

- 🧹 Cleaning
- ❄️ AC installation and repairs
- 🔧 Handyman services
- ⚡ Electrical services
- 🚰 Plumbing
- 🏗️ Construction
- 🛠️ Repairs and maintenance
- And other local services

## 🎯 MVP Vision

The goal of Servio is to build a simple foundation for a multi-service marketplace without trying to solve everything at once.

The MVP will focus on the core user experience:

1. **Discover** available services
2. **Browse** service providers
3. **View** provider and service details
4. **Request or book** a service
5. **Manage** bookings
6. **Review** completed services

The application will remain intentionally lightweight at this stage, with more advanced marketplace features introduced as the product evolves.

## 🧩 Core Concept

**People need things done. Professionals have the skills to do them. Servio connects the two.**

```text
Customer
   ↓
Find a service
   ↓
Browse providers
   ↓
Choose a provider
   ↓
Request / Book
   ↓
Service completed
   ↓
Review
```
